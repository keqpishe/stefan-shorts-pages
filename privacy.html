Privacy Policy

Stefan Shorts Uploader is a personal application used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The application requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos and read basic channel information. It does not collect, store, sell or share data of any other person. The authorization token is stored locally on the owner's computer and is not transmitted to third parties.

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
